Our Mission
01 — Authenticity

Delivering a real experience.

We did not merely simplify natural experiences. We optimised them. Our team distilled the very essence of nature from the wild forests of Northern Sweden. What you experience is not an approximation. It is a truth.

02 — Efficiency

Promoting wellbeing, efficiently.

We aim to achieve the highest possible impact on your wellbeing in the shortest possible time. We are not asking you to find time for nature, we will bring nature into the time you already have.

03 — Humanity

With a human-centered approach.

We aim to create a natural experience that for the first time is truly designed for the human user. No more going out into the forest like a caveman. Finally, nature made fit for the modern age.

04 — Ubiquity

Available worldwide on demand.

We believe our emulator is of value to anyone living in the modern world. Contact us for inquiries. We are serious about this.
